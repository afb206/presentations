# Data provenance and handling

Data files are **not stored in this repository** (size, licences, and the repository must stay private). They are held on Arrakis in `~/Datasets/PlantGrowthData/` and in `data/data-raw/`.

- `data/data-raw/` holds symbolic links to three raw downloads (`FIP1_Data`, `Maize_Dryad_Data`, `Arabidopsis`) and, for the three datasets added later, the downloads themselves: `WiDiv_Drone_Height/` (a git clone), `Anderson_maize_height/` and `Sorghum_field_scanner/`. These links and folders exist on Arrakis only. On another machine, download the sources below and place them under the same folder names.
- `data/data-input/` and `data/data-valid/` are generated by the scripts in `code/` (`01_data-prep-wheat.R`, `01_data-prep-maize.R`, `01_data-prep-arabidopsis.R`, `01_data-prep-widiv.R`, `01_data-prep-anderson.R`, `01_data-prep-sorghum.R`). They are ignored by git.
- Earlier hand-saved copies remain in `~/Datasets/PlantGrowthData/processed_data/`. The wheat, maize and Arabidopsis scripts were checked against them in October 2026 (row counts and values matched). The WiDiv script was checked against a hand-saved copy in `processed_data/` in the project root (same names and values). Anderson and sorghum have no earlier saved copy to check against.
- Each script stops with an error if a row count differs from the verified value. Counts that were printed but not asserted are noted in the entries below (sorghum plot means, Anderson entry types).
- "Downloaded" dates below are file timestamps on Arrakis, not recorded download dates.

## Wheat: FIP 1.0 plant height (ETH Zürich)

- **Source:** FIP 1.0 data set, ETH Zürich field phenotyping platform. Data note: "The FIP 1.0 Data Set: Highly Resolved Annotated Image Time Series of 4,000 Wheat Plots Grown in Six Years" (PMC12153353; preprint bioRxiv 10.1101/2024.10.04.616624). Trait CSVs exported from CroPyDB via libdrive.ethz.ch, not the image-bundled copy.
- **Downloaded:** 13 August 2026 (file timestamps)
- **Used:** plant height (`trait_id` 5; methods 302 = TLS, 304 = drone) from 15 trait files, plus 14 design files joined on `plot.UID`.
- **Script and outputs:** `01_data-prep-wheat.R` writes `data-input/wheat_height_input.rds` (142,646 rows) and `data-valid/wheat_height_valid.rds` (137,762 rows).
- **Handling notes:**
  - `measurement_id` identifies a measurement session, not a row. (`plot.UID`, `timestamp`, `method.id`) is unique.
  - FPWW022_lot3 has TLS and drone measurements. TLS measurements are dropped on days when a drone measurement exists in the same lot (5 of 7 TLS dates).
  - FPWW028 (small 2019 lot) is excluded wholesale.
  - Six FPWW012 plants flagged by the gap between raw peak height and the reference final height (trait 119) are removed. **Provisional:** the plant IDs are fixed in the script, the cutoff is a judgement, and the gap calculation is not yet rebuilt from the raw trait 119 files (decision issue #16).
  - Canopy cover is not used (senescence-driven artefact; decision issue #18).
  - Check plots (`spatial_check` = 1) are replicated far more than non-check genotypes (roughly 9-12x, from earlier exploration).
- **Can it be shared:** TBC (check the licence on the ETH record)

## Maize: Ames NDVI panel

- **Source:** Dryad, "Data and scripts for: Genetic dissection of seasonal vegetation index dynamics in maize through aerial based high-throughput phenotyping", doi:10.5061/dryad.44j0zpcf0 (Feb 2022 version). Paper: Wang et al., *The Plant Genome* 14(3): e20155 (2021), doi:10.1002/tpg2.20155. Manual browser download (Dryad's API needs authentication).
- **Downloaded:** 13 August 2026 (file timestamps)
- **Used:** only `Pspline_Modeling_NDVI_growth/` (NDVI observations and the published P-spline parameters and daily predictions). 1,752 accessions, 5 timepoints (37, 44, 60, 73, 115 DAP).
- **Script and outputs:** `01_data-prep-maize.R` writes three files to `data-input/`: `maize_ndvi_long.rds` (8,760 rows), `maize_pspline_params.rds` (1,752 rows), `maize_pspline_predictions_long.rds` (138,329 rows).
- **Handling notes:**
  - `AmesDP2017_NDVI_long` is tab-delimited with no file extension. The row counts in the Dryad README are misleading.
  - The prediction file holds 79 daily values per genotype joined with `_`; the script splits them (DAP 37-115).
  - Accession NSL437884 has the text "FALSE" in place of predictions. It has no benchmark predictions but keeps its NDVI observations and parameters.
  - NDVI is a bounded greenness index, not a size measure, so growth rates from it are not true AGR or RGR.
- **Can it be shared:** TBC (check the Dryad record)

## Arabidopsis: rosette area (IPK Gatersleben)

- **Source:** e!DAL-PGP, IPK Gatersleben: "Image analysis results from quantitative monitoring of 484 Arabidopsis thaliana plants using high-throughput plant phenotyping" (A. Junker, 2016); workbook `1135FA_analysis.xlsx`. The record gives CC BY 4.0. **DOI/URL: TBC** (confirm that the workbook belongs to this record).
- **Downloaded:** 13 August 2026 (file timestamp)
- **Used:** sheet "Analysis Results", `Row Type` = "top", trait `area (zoom-corrected geometry trait based on visible-light top view) [mm^2]`. Days 1-44.
- **Script and outputs:** `01_data-prep-arabidopsis.R` writes `data-input/arabidopsis_rosette_area_input.rds` (7,119 rows) and `data-valid/arabidopsis_rosette_area_valid.rds` (5,545 rows, 425 plants).
- **Handling notes:**
  - The sheet has no genotype or treatment values for plant rows. The record describes the genotype as C24.
  - 485 plant IDs include one leaked row (`1135FA`, the experiment label), removed first.
  - `group` (moving / stationary) is **reconstructed**, not recorded: plants with fewer than 10 observations are labelled stationary (imaged about every 4 days from about day 24). This gives 384 moving and 100 stationary, matching the paper.
  - Exclusions: 51 dead or failed-germination plants (peak area below 100 mm^2); days 29 and 41 (elevated within-plant crash rates); 8 stunted plants (peak area below 500 mm^2, at a natural gap in the sorted values).
  - Plant `1135FA1080` is labelled "not specified (group 00)" on 19 of its rows. It is kept.
- **Can it be shared:** CC BY 4.0 per the record (confirm it covers the workbook)

## Maize: WiDiv plot height, 2019 (University of Minnesota)

- **Source:** GitHub, `HirschLabUMN/WiDiv_Drone_Height`, cloned at commit `8fed4415f99f7ee4f8e43fb088319622bedc81a3`. Paper: Sweet et al., "Temporally resolved growth patterns reveal novel information about the polygenic nature of complex quantitative traits", *The Plant Journal* (2024), doi:10.1111/tpj.17092. The authors' deposit is at DRUM, doi:10.13020/SKJN-QX31 (not downloaded; the GitHub `Raw_Data/` was used instead).
- **Downloaded:** October 2026 (cloned with git; exact date not recorded, the commit above pins the version)
- **Used:** `Raw_Data/2019/` only: 21 flight files in `Data_Analysis/Height/Means/WIDIV/` (one unlabelled column of plot heights each), the plot order, the ground control point (GCP) pixel files in `All_Pixels/GCP/` with the known target heights in `Manual_GCP.csv`, and stand counts, plus `Raw_Data/All/Plots2GenotypeAllYears.csv`. Plot-level data, 1,040 plots, 501 genotypes.
- **Script and outputs:** `01_data-prep-widiv.R` writes `data-input/widiv_height_2019_input.rds` (21,840 rows: 21 flights x 1,040 plots, raw units), `data-valid/widiv_height_2019_valid.rds` (10,020 rows, 1,002 plots, 493 genotypes, 10 flights, 9,541 non-missing heights) and `data-valid/widiv_height_2019_flight_scales.rds` (21 rows).
- **Handling notes:**
  - Plot names come from `Plots_WIDIV.txt`, matched to the flight values by position (every flight file has 1,056 lines). The 16 non-field rows (all named "PH207") are dropped.
  - Raw heights are on an arbitrary scale that differs between flights (the elevation model is an 8-bit image). Each flight is rescaled with the GCPs: the extracted target height is the maximum minus the minimum of the pixel values over the target, the scale is the known height (ft) over the extracted height, and `height_ft` = raw x scale / 100 (plot values carry a factor of 100 that the pixel files do not). The `Means/GCP` files are not used (the authors say the plot method is too inaccurate for small targets).
  - **Provisional:** the authors chose which targets to trust by eye and that file is not in the repository. The script keeps the targets within 25% of the flight median, needs at least 3, and averages them.
  - **Provisional:** flights before 2 July (no plants yet) and 19 July (only 2 agreeing targets) are dropped, and so are 10 and 12 July (heights rise on 10 July then fall below earlier flights on 12 July, judged from plot-level lines).
  - Other exclusions: 26 plots with a stand count under 10; interior dips and peaks set to missing (373 dips, 143 peaks, 479 values missing after the plot drop below); 12 plots flagged 3 or more times dropped. The authors' last-flight rule is not applied, because it would delete the late-season decline.
  - B73 and PH207 are the checks: 20 plots each, against 2 for most lines.
  - **Not validated:** the absolute height level. The 2019 manual measurements are on plots the paper says are not study plots, so the 2021 manual measurements are the planned check.
  - The repository has 21 of the paper's 27 flights for 2019. The paper says four 2019 flights were dropped but not which. 2018, 2020 and 2021 are not processed. The 2019 lodging event the paper mentions is not investigated.
  - No decision issue raised yet for the provisional rules.
- **Can it be shared:** TBC (check the DRUM record and the GitHub repository licence)

## Maize: Anderson UAS plant height, 2018 (Texas A&M)

- **Source:** Dryad, doi:10.5061/dryad.q573n5tf2, `PhenotypeFiles.zip`. Paper: Anderson et al., "Unoccupied aerial system enabled functional modeling of maize height reveals dynamic expression of loci", *Plant Direct* 4 (2020), doi:10.1002/pld3.223. Manual browser download.
- **Downloaded:** TBC (file timestamp not checked)
- **Used:** `CS18-YYCI_Heightscompiled.csv` (irrigated) and `CS18-YYCD_Heightscompiled.csv` (non-irrigated), the v1 files. 1,216 plots per environment: three RIL populations plus parents and checks.
- **Script and outputs:** `01_data-prep-anderson.R` writes `data-input/anderson_maize_height_input.rds` (43,776 rows) and `data-valid/anderson_maize_height_valid.rds` (16,987 rows, 7 flights).
- **Handling notes:**
  - The heights are the 95th percentile of plot canopy height (P95, metres) from drone images. The v2 files add fake rows at 0 to 15 days after sowing (`Estimate` = 0, `Points` = 3000) that are anchors for the authors' Weibull fits, so they are not used. The authors' BLUPs, Weibull parameters and imputed daily heights are model output and are not used.
  - 768 heights of exactly 0 are set to missing (all have 3 or fewer points in the plot cloud).
  - The flights kept are the authors' quality-controlled flights (43, 57, 62, 65, 69, 100, 117 DAS), without 35 DAS (most plots have no usable point cloud). The other flights in the files were left out by the authors and some are inconsistent (64 DAS reads 0.49 m between 0.87 m at 62 and 1.03 m at 65). 37 rows with no height are dropped.
  - Dense checks: RIL entries have 2 plots each, parents and checks 18 to 36 (about 9 to 18 times). `entry_type` labels entries with more than 4 plots as `check/parent`. The entry-type counts are printed, not asserted. Y290 (a RIL) has 1 plot irrigated; PB80 (6 plots, irrigated only) is treated as a check/parent.
  - Heights are biased low (asymptote about 1.1 m against manual terminal heights of 1.6 m, which the paper puts down to the 120 m flight altitude). Use shape and relative rates, not absolute height.
  - **Platform:** the 100 and 117 DAS flights have far fewer points per plot (about 600 against 1,000 to 12,000), which fits the paper's fixed-wing flights at 120 m. The `Platform` column says "UASFusion" for every row, so `platform_inferred` (DJI before 100 DAS, Tuffwing from 100) is inferred. It is fully confounded with the plateau phase. Against the 69 DAS flight, the median ratio is 0.93 to 1.06, but plot-level agreement is only moderate (r about 0.6).
  - 7 flights per plot, 5 of them on the rise (the first near the noise floor), so the data are sparse.
  - No decision issue raised yet for the platform step.
- **Can it be shared:** TBC (check the Dryad record)

## Sorghum: Field Scanner EMS population, 2020 (University of Arizona)

- **Source:** CyVerse Data Commons, doi:10.25739/zpnk-9v06 (curated folder `Demieville_sorghumDataNote_2025`). Data note: Demieville et al. 2025, "High-resolution phenomics dataset collected on a field-grown, EMS-mutagenized sorghum population evaluated in hot, arid conditions" (PMC12308891). The Data Commons website needed a login and blocked the IP, so the files were fetched through anonymous WebDAV at `data.cyverse.org/dav-anon/iplant/commons/cyverse_curated/Demieville_sorghumDataNote_2025/`.
- **Downloaded:** 20 October 2026 (file timestamps): `2020_full.zip`, `2022_full.zip`, `header_information.tsv`, `ReadMe.txt`. The cleaned zips and the weather zip were not downloaded.
- **Used:** `2020_full/2020_full/2020_full_RGB.csv` only: 324,048 rows, 9,854 plant IDs, 512 genotypes, 55 scan dates (5 to 135 days after planting), well watered (WW) and water limited (WL), all plots type `experimental`. The 3D file (20 dates, to 49 DAP), the FLIR and PSII files are not used.
- **Script and outputs:** `01_data-prep-sorghum.R` writes `data-input/sorghum_fs_2020_rgb_input.rds` (324,048 rows) and two files to `data-valid/`: `sorghum_fs_2020_rgb_plants_valid.rds` (248,543 rows, 5,283 plants, 51 dates) and `sorghum_fs_2020_rgb_plotmeans_valid.rds` (68,189 rows, 1,499 plots, 47 dates). The plot-means counts are printed, not asserted.
- **Handling notes:**
  - `bounding_area_m2` is the area of the box around each plant detection, so it is a size proxy that overstates sprawling or touching plants, not leaf area. There is no height and no biomass in the RGB file.
  - The full file is used, not the authors' `cleaned` file. Their cleaning (plants detected on at least 67% of days, plus Tukey-fence outlier removal) leaves 5,545 plants, and the outlier step did not reduce scan-to-scan jumpiness (12.0% of jumps beyond 1.5x after 70 days, against 12.6% here).
  - Four partial-scan dates are dropped for all plants (44, 58, 86 and 114 DAP; 5,975 rows). They scanned far fewer plants than the usual 6,000 to 7,000.
  - The plant-level table keeps plants seen on at least 40 of the 51 remaining dates. Plant IDs fragment: there are 6.6 IDs per plot against 5 plants by design, with overlapping date ranges, so identity is uncertain even for the kept plants (601 of 1,371 plots have exactly 5 kept IDs, at most 6).
  - The plot-level table starts at 12 DAP, because earlier scans detect only the larger plants. It uses all detections, so `n_detections` can exceed 5 (median 5).
  - Noise is heavy tailed (12.6% of consecutive scans after 70 DAP change by more than 1.5x), so use a heavy-tailed likelihood when modelling. WW and WL diverge after about day 55. There are no check plots in 2020.
  - **2022 was examined and is not used.** The 2022 file has 157,815 plant IDs with a median of 3 scans each, one ID named "double" with 136,369 rows (14% of the file, probably merged detections), a median of 13 detections per plot per date against 5 plants, `rep` all missing, and a `transition` plot type with treatment WL/WW.
- **Can it be shared:** TBC (check the CyVerse record)
